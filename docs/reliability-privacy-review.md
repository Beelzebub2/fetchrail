## Current primary-source checks

* [Portugal consolidated Law 41/2004, Art. 5](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/lei/2004-106523049): prior informed consent; narrow transmission/strictly necessary service exceptions. The live consolidated page lists last amendment 16 August 2022. Local generation does not automatically exempt later retrieval: [EDPB final 2/2023, adopted 7 October 2024, published 16 October 2024](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/guideline/guidelines-22023-on-technical-scope-of-art-53-of-eprivacy-directive_en).
* [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), especially Arts. 5–7, 13, 25, 28, 30, 32–36, 44–49, and [EDPB consent 05/2020](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/guideline/guidelines-052020-on-consent-under-regulation-2016679_en): specific affirmative choices, no detriment for refusal, easy withdrawal; controller accountability persists after aggregation.
* [CNPD DPIA guidance and Regulation 798/2018](https://www.cnpd.pt/organizacoes/outras-obrigacoes/avaliacao-de-impacto/): screen likely high-risk processing and the mandatory list. No intended profiling/significant automated decisions, large-scale special-category processing or public monitoring here. This is a preliminary screen; the controller must assess actual scale, children, sensitive download inference, rare cells and residual risk. Consent does not remove DPIA duties. Prior consultation applies if high residual risk remains.
* Rechecked 18 October: [CNPD rights](https://www.cnpd.pt/cidadaos/direitos/) and [complaint channel](https://www.cnpd.pt/cidadaos/participacoes/). The notices now state the consent basis, withdrawal, applicable rights and supervisory complaint route. The operator must process requests within one month, explain any permitted extension within that month, avoid unnecessary identity data and never request private download contents to locate an unidentified aggregate contribution.
* [Cloudflare DPA](https://www.cloudflare.com/en-gb/cloudflare-customer-dpa/), live version 6.4 effective 3 April 2026, and [SCCs](https://www.cloudflare.com/en-gb/cloudflare-customer-scc/): website availability is not evidence the account accepted these terms or completed a transfer assessment. Record accepted contract/version, subprocessors, roles, safeguards and rights assistance before release.
* [DO data location](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/reference/data-location/) and [regional processing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/data-localization/how-to/durable-objects/): EU object jurisdiction is a different identity and does not constrain every Worker/metadata path. Keep existing daily-v1 storage in place until an authorized migration decision. No EU-only claim.

A daily accepted-batch cap cannot protect the shared account from arbitrary rejected requests; production needs provider-side abuse controls and monitored quotas.
