## Capacity and abuse

The coordinator shares a 2,000 accepted batches/hour and 20,000/UTC-day budget across v1, hardware summaries, v2 and v3. At most 64 dimension rows/day across all three aggregate tables survive admission; 30 receipt-days gives at most 1,920 aggregate rows. V3 also retains nonce/hash receipts for eight days and daily ingestion counters for 30 days. Five-batch suppression is not a people count. The v3 one-bit clipping flag counts affected report batches; legacy clipping meanings remain separate. Nonce deduplication prevents unchanged retries from being counted again only while their receipts survive; fresh forged batches and declared provenance remain unauthenticated.

V1/v2 acceptance updates an aggregate and two budget rows. V3 adds a deduplication receipt: **at least 80,000 logical row writes/day at an all-v3 daily cap**, before index work, TTL deletes, duplicate/rejection diagnostics, alarms and other account workloads. Accepted diagnostics are derived from existing aggregates to avoid another write. This floor is not provider billing metering. The earlier 19 October account planning used documented Free allowances; the [current provider plan and pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/platform/pricing/) and actual account usage must be checked before using those figures as available headroom. In particular, 80,000 logical mutations plus indexing and expiry must not be assumed to fit a 100,000-row allowance. Preserve admission limits during this change, meter actual amplification, and agree an operating budget/alert or appropriate plan before increasing traffic. Rejected requests also spend invocation/SQL work. Provider abuse controls must not introduce device fingerprints or application IP retention.

## Withdrawal, deletion, restore and rollback

Withdrawal immediately invalidates the current consent epoch, clears unsent native memory, cancels in-flight client work and deletes the recognized v3 outbox and validated private-atomic staging copies; a body already received cannot be recalled. The queue is bounded to 64 batches / 256 KiB of serialized plaintext, including local metadata, and seven days; encoding adds file overhead. It retries immutable batches with their original nonce. A fresh grant cannot revive an older grant's queue, and no restored/history/pre-consent transfer is enrolled later. New scope choices use separate protected consent receipts; stale settings cannot override a saved withdrawal. Inaccessible or damaged storage can prevent deletion, so the UI exposes a safe error and repeated off choices retry cleanup. A closed app performs expiry on its next start. Local delivery status exposes queue, retry, expiry, overflow and permanent-rejection counts without paths or identifiers.

Optional protected local breadcrumbs retain at most 20 cases/seven days and 512 KiB plaintext, only after their separate local choice. Cleanup runs on startup/inspection/periodic saving; a closed app cannot erase an expired file. Deletion verifies an owned schema/purpose and preserves an unknown/corrupt file for manual inspection. No backup generations are created. User-exported files remain where the user saves them until the user deletes them.

Application aggregate rows and daily ingestion counters expire after 30 UTC receipt-days; v3 nonce/hash receipts expire after eight days. The hourly alarm and every report/collect prune all relevant tables. On restore, run expiry before serving reads; a lost receipt can weaken retry deduplication. [Cloudflare SQLite PITR](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/api/sqlite-storage-api/) can retain recovery history for up to 30 further days and is unavailable in local development; verify actual restoration and processor deletion behavior separately. Do not promise immediate erasure of unidentified contributions after aggregation or of provider recovery/security copies.

For a v3 rollback, close `QUALITY_V3_ANALYTICS_ENABLED` while retaining the compatible receiver, existing v1/v2 behavior, private reads and all expiry code. New clients treat the closed gate's 503 as retryable within the bounded queue. **Do not roll back to a v2-only Worker that rejects v3 and never prunes its new tables** while v3 rows or recovery copies remain: that can permanently reject queued reports and abandon cleanup. Retain client withdrawal controls and use an explicit client-compatibility/retention plan for any deeper rollback. No automatic jurisdiction migration or table deletion belongs in a rollback.

No remote incident database or upload route is included. Safe local export is the incident workflow. A future confidential support intake needs a demonstrated need, separately reviewed storage/access/retention/erasure and fresh per-incident action before sending any report.
